# Keep Agentville up to date

You manage Agentville in **Customize**, then the **Plugins** tab.

## Get updates

- Turn on **Sync automatically** to get new versions without doing anything.
- Or use **Check for updates** whenever you want the newest version now.

## Pause it

Use the **Disable plugin** toggle to turn Agentville off for a while. While it is off, take the two lines out of your Instructions too, or Claude will look for a plugin that is not there.

## Remove it

Use **Remove** to take Agentville away completely, then delete the two lines from your Instructions.

## Switch between Stable and beta

Only one version can be on at a time, because they clash. Remove the one you have, then add the other from the **Discover** tab.
